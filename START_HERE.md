# Start here: plain English guide

This page explains Asso Lab without assuming you know SYSTASYS, Asso, AI governance, or any historical project name.

## The simple idea

AI agents should not only produce answers. They should leave evidence.

Asso Lab is a small public proof surface derived from the broader Asso / SYSTASYS work. It shows how AI-assisted work can be bounded, reviewed and recorded through receipts.

Some older files use the ACE name. That is historical provenance, not the current parent identity of this repository or of SYSTASYS.

## What problem does this solve?

AI systems often say things like:

> Done.

But a human reviewer still needs to know:

- What was done?
- What was not done?
- Which sources were used?
- Was there a receipt?
- Was anything sent, published, deployed, traded or executed?
- Can the result be reviewed later?

Asso Lab exists to make those questions easier to answer.

## What is a receipt?

A receipt is a small evidence record.

It can contain fields such as status, timestamp, sources, hash, signature, what happened and what did not happen.

A receipt is not a marketing claim. It is something a reviewer can inspect.

## Everyday analogy

Imagine someone says:

> I prepared the report.

A receipt is the trace that lets you check which report, when, from which sources, whether it was only drafted or actually sent, and who still needs to review it.

That is the difference between a claim and evidence.

## What Asso Lab shows publicly

This repository shows:

- public briefings;
- receipts attached to those briefings;
- public-safe proof and validation material;
- doctrine explaining why AI agents should stay bounded;
- examples of action receipts, including refused actions;
- dated historical artifacts when their provenance is useful.

## What Asso Lab does not show

This repository does not expose the current private SYSTASYS runtime.

It does not grant agents permission to act.

It does not prove production readiness or current terrain state.

It is a public proof surface, not a live operations mirror.
