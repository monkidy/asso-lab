# CLAUDE.md: Asso Lab public repository

Read this before changing the repository.

## Current role

Asso Lab is a bounded public proof surface derived from the broader Asso / SYSTASYS work.

It is not the current SYSTASYS runtime, not a current-state owner, not a social publishing control plane, and not a mirror of private operations.

## Truth boundary

```text
PUBLIC_ARTIFACT != PRIVATE_RUNTIME
HISTORICAL_NAME != CURRENT_PARENT_IDENTITY
RECEIPT != AUTHORITY
README_CLAIM != TERRAIN_PROOF
```

Some dated files preserve the ACE name. Keep that provenance when it matters, but do not use ACE as the current parent identity of Asso or SYSTASYS.

## Public repo rules

- Keep the repository reader-first and evidence-first.
- Do not add secrets, credentials, private logs, personal calibration or private operator state.
- Do not add current broker, wallet, infrastructure or economic state.
- Do not reintroduce the old social publishing pipeline, scheduler, lead-generation routines or private communication automation.
- Do not claim current SYSTASYS runtime or terrain status from this repository.
- Keep examples clearly separated from live proof.
- Prefer small, inspectable public artifacts over broad internal mirrors.
- Preserve historical provenance without presenting old labels as current architecture.

## What belongs here

- public-safe doctrine;
- public briefs and receipts;
- static examples;
- public-safe validators;
- explanatory diagrams;
- dated historical artifacts that remain useful as provenance.

## What does not belong here

- private SYSTASYS source or runtime;
- credentials or secret-bearing configuration;
- personal operating dossiers;
- current internal control surfaces;
- live publishing automation;
- current financial or broker state;
- instructions that grant authority.

## Change discipline

Every material change should keep the public/private boundary explicit and should be reviewable from the diff.

Hichem remains the final human authority for public identity and publication decisions.
