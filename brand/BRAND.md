# Brand kit: ACE / Asso Lab

> **Historical brand snapshot.** This file preserves the visual language used by the public proof surface in June 2026. It is not the current cross-SYSTASYS brand canon and must not be used as authority for current SYSTASYS, Asso or site identity.

The visual identity used for the early ACE / Asso Lab / Proof of Agent public surface was built around one idea: verifiable proof.

Ink on paper, monospace, a verification seal. Serious and technical, never flashy.

## Concept

"The proof layer for AI agents." Everything looks dated, signed and auditable.

## Colors

- Ink: `#111111` (primary text, marks)
- Paper: `#FAFAF7` (background)
- Signal green: `#1FA463` (verified, allow, pass)
- Refused red: `#DC2626` (deny, fail-closed, reserved for blocked states)

Rule: green means allowed or verified, red means refused. The two-state accent mirrors the gate doctrine.

## Type

- Monospace for marks, hashes and receipts: JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono.
- Sans for body and headlines: Inter.

## Historical marks

- Wordmark: `[ ACE ]` with green brackets.
- Seal: stamped double ring with a green check, used as the early "Proof of Agent" signature mark.
